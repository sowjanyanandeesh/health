Sleep Hygiene & Hydration Tips

Quality rest and optimal fluid intake form the backbone of physical and mental performance.

Hydration Rules

  • Target 2.5–3.5 Liters Daily: Adjust based on activity level, body size, and local climate.
  • Morning Hydration: Drink 16 oz of fresh water immediately upon waking to kickstart digestion.
  • Electrolytes Matter: Add a pinch of sea salt or lemon during heavy workouts to retain essential mineral balance.

Sleep Essentials

  • 7–9 Hours Rule: Consistent sleep schedules optimize brain function, cell repair, and hormone regulation.
  • No Screens 60 Mins Before Bed: Blue light suppresses natural melatonin synthesis needed for deep sleep.
  • Cool Environment: Maintain a room temperature around 65°F (18°C) for optimal rapid eye movement (REM) cycles.
